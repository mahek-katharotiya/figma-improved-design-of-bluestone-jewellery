## Experiment 1: Interface Analysis & Usability Benchmarking
A comparative UX evaluation of leading Indian jewellery e-commerce platforms 
(**GIVA, BlueStone, PALMONAS, Tanishq**) across 18 standard usability parameters and 
**Shneiderman’s 8 Golden Rules**. The study analyzes trade-offs between feature density and 
minimalist browsing, establishing baseline benchmarks to eliminate promotional clutter, 
simplify complex category hierarchies, and improve mobile responsiveness.

## Experiment 2: User Research, Personas & Journey Mapping
Qualitative behavioral research mapping customer workflows across six purchasing stages—from discovery
to post-purchase. Identifies empathy maps and core customer archetypes (**Gen Z, Gift Seekers, NRIs, 
Family Buyers, Senior Citizens**) to resolve critical e-commerce friction points surrounding online sizing anxiety,
gold price/purity transparency, and cross-border returns.
